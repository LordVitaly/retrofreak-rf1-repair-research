# Safety and sensitive-data reporting

This archive documents work on one physical device. It does not provide a warranty,
universal firmware, a safe electrical wiring specification for every revision, or
a verified automatic rollback.

Do not attach private images, keys, serials or full unredacted logs to public issues.
A public issue may describe a documentation defect or unsafe instruction without
including sensitive data. No private security-response service or response-time
commitment is implied; use a maintainer-enabled private reporting channel if one
is actually available. Do not invent a contact address.

Corrections to unsafe documentation take precedence over preserving a flattering
repair narrative. See [Safety and scope](docs/SAFETY_AND_SCOPE.md),
[Privacy](PRIVACY.md), and [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
